Privacy Policy

This diagnostic collects the contact details and assessment answers you choose to submit so Mareva Etiko can show results, prepare follow-up analysis, and contact you about relevant ESG and AI readiness services.

What is collected

Name, work email, organization, diagnostic answers, score, dimension results, and locally saved assessment history.

How it is used

To generate the report card, support follow-up conversations, improve the diagnostic, and prepare audit-readiness recommendations.

Local storage

The browser stores recent assessment history locally for comparison and the local admin dashboard. Clearing browser storage removes that local history.

Third parties

The site does not sell personal information. If a backend, Google Sheets, or email workflow is connected, submitted data may be processed by those services for lead capture and reporting operations.

Email hello@marevaetiko.com for privacy or data questions.
