Diagnostic Disclaimer

The Mareva Etiko ESG x AI Readiness Diagnostic is an informational self-assessment. It is designed to identify likely readiness gaps, evidence needs, and audit-preparation priorities.

Not assurance or legal advice

The diagnostic is not a statutory audit, assurance engagement, certification, legal opinion, or regulatory compliance determination. Organizations should consult qualified legal, audit, and assurance professionals before relying on results for formal reporting decisions.

Self-reported answers

Scores are based on user-provided answers. A formal audit-readiness review should validate documents, controls, source data, ownership, and evidence trails before conclusions are used externally.

Illustrative benchmarks

Benchmark ranges and maturity labels are directional examples. They are not claims about named organizations and are not statistically validated population averages.

Recommended next step

Use this report card as a scoping tool for a deeper evidence review, controls assessment, and 90-day implementation roadmap.
